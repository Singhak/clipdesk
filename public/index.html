How it works

From screen recording to Reel in three steps

The old way meant a video editor, keyframes and a lot of cropping. ClipDesk does that part for you.

1

Pick a style

Choose a ready-made template for coding tutorials, product demos, presentations or browser walkthroughs.

2

Record

Share your screen, add your camera and mic if you want, and hit record. A countdown gives you time to get ready.

3

Download your vertical video

Trim the start and end, add captions, and save an MP4 that is ready to upload.

Features

Everything a screen Reel needs

Built for founders, developers and teachers who want to show their work, not learn editing software.

🎯

Camera follows your cursor

The 9:16 frame glides to wherever you are working, so small text stays readable on a phone.

🔍

Zoom on clicks

Clicks trigger a smooth zoom, so viewers see exactly what you pressed.

💬

Captions without an API key

Word-by-word captions are generated on your device with Whisper. Import or export .srt and .vtt files too.

🧑‍💻

Camera bubble

A round webcam overlay, with on-device background blur or replacement.

🎙️

Clean voice audio

A noise gate, voice EQ and compressor make a laptop mic sound much better.

🔒

Private by design

Recording, rendering and captions all happen on your computer. Your video is never uploaded.

Questions

Frequently asked


Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, rendering and caption generation all run on your computer. The only network use is a one-time download of the captions model (about 40 MB), and checking your licence key.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. The desktop app is Windows only for now. The web app and Chrome extension work on any desktop browser.

Why use the desktop app instead of the browser?

The desktop app can record any program, such as VS Code, Figma or a terminal, follow your cursor across them, capture system audio and keep its own controls out of the video. The web app can only react to your mouse over its own preview, so it can’t follow or zoom while you work in another app.

Is ClipDesk open source?

Yes, it is released under GPL-3.0. Pro pays for the convenience and the limits being lifted, and it funds development.

What happens when my Pro pass ends?

You drop back to Free. Your existing videos are untouched. You can renew any time using the same key.
