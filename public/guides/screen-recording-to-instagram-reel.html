How to turn a screen recording into an Instagram Reel

A normal screen recording is 16:9 landscape, but Instagram Reels are 9:16 vertical. Post a landscape recording as-is and it shrinks to a thin strip with black bars, and your text is unreadable on a phone. Here is how to make a screen recording into a proper Instagram Reel without opening Premiere, CapCut or any timeline editor.

Short answer: record with ClipDesk. It is a free, open-source screen recorder that crops to vertical 9:16 while you record, follows your cursor, zooms on clicks and adds captions, so you get a 1080×1920 MP4 ready for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Nothing is uploaded.

Step by step

  1. Open ClipDesk. Download the Windows app, add the Chrome/Edge extension, or open the web app in your browser. All three make the same 1080×1920 video.
  2. Pick a template. Choose a ready-made style such as coding tutorial, product demo, presentation or browser walkthrough. The layout is set to 9:16 for Reels.
  3. Record your screen. Share your screen or window, add your camera and mic if you like, and press record. A countdown gives you time to get ready. Work normally: the vertical frame follows your cursor and zooms when you click.
  4. Trim and add captions. Cut out mistakes from the start, end or middle. Generate word-by-word captions on your device with no API key. Most Reel viewers watch with the sound off, so captions matter.
  5. Download the MP4 and upload to Instagram. Save the 1080×1920 MP4, then upload it to Instagram as a Reel from your phone or from the Instagram website.

What size is an Instagram Reel?

Instagram Reels are vertical, 9:16, and 1080×1920 pixels is the standard size. ClipDesk exports exactly that, as an H.264/AAC MP4 that Instagram accepts.

Why screen recordings look bad as Reels

A 1920×1080 recording shown in a 9:16 frame is only about a third of the height, and small UI text becomes unreadable. The fix is to crop to a vertical window that moves to where you are working. That is the "follow the cursor" camera in ClipDesk, plus a zoom on clicks so viewers see what you pressed.

Tips for Reels that get watched

Keep it short: the Free plan records up to 90 seconds, which suits most Reels. Show the result in the first two seconds. Turn on captions. Use the camera bubble if you want a face on screen, and keep the mic clean with the built-in noise gate and voice EQ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make Instagram Reels from my laptop screen for free?

Yes. ClipDesk is free and open source. The Free plan exports 1080×1920 video with captions, camera bubble and trimming, limited to 90 seconds with a ClipDesk watermark. Pro removes the watermark and the limit.

Is the video uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, rendering and captions all happen on your computer, so your screen is never sent to a server.

Do I need a video editor?

No. Cursor follow, click zoom and the 9:16 crop are automatic. You only trim the take and add captions if you want them.
