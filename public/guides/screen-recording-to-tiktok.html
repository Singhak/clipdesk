How to turn a screen recording into a TikTok video

TikTok is vertical, and a landscape screen recording looks tiny and letterboxed there. If you make software demos, coding clips, design walkthroughs or tutorials, you want the part of the screen you are working on to fill the 9:16 frame. This guide shows how to make a TikTok from a screen recording automatically.

Short answer: record with ClipDesk. It is a free, open-source screen recorder that crops to vertical 9:16 while you record, follows your cursor, zooms on clicks and adds captions, so you get a 1080×1920 MP4 ready for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Nothing is uploaded.

Step by step

  1. Open ClipDesk. Pick the Windows app, the Chrome/Edge extension or the web app.
  2. Pick a style. Choose a template for tutorials, demos, presentations or browser walkthroughs.
  3. Record your screen. Press record and talk through what you are doing. Optional: add a round camera bubble with background blur so viewers see you too.
  4. Add captions and trim. Generate animated word-by-word captions on your device, and cut dead air from the start, end or middle.
  5. Export and post. Download the 1080×1920 MP4 and upload it to TikTok. The same file also works for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Best size for TikTok screen recordings

TikTok uses 9:16 vertical video, and 1080×1920 is the standard. ClipDesk exports that directly, so there is no re-cropping or scaling afterwards.

Why captions and zoom matter on TikTok

Many viewers scroll with the sound off and watch on a small screen. Word-by-word captions keep them watching, and zooming on clicks makes sure they can see what you pressed.

Privacy

ClipDesk records, renders and captions on your own computer and does not upload your video. The only downloads are the one-time captions model (about 40 MB) and licence checks for Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Can I record my laptop screen for TikTok?

Yes. Use the Windows app for any screen or app window, the extension for a browser tab, or the web app in Chrome or Edge.

Does ClipDesk work on Mac?

The desktop app is Windows only for now. The web app and Chrome/Edge extension work on any desktop browser, but they can only follow your cursor while you are inside the recorded page or ClipDesk's preview.

Is there a watermark?

The Free plan adds a small ClipDesk watermark and limits recordings to 90 seconds. Pro (₹499 for 30 days or ₹2,999 lifetime) removes both.
