ClipDesk guides

How to turn a screen recording into a vertical video for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok, using the free, open-source ClipDesk recorder.

How to turn a screen recording into an Instagram Reel
Turn a widescreen screen recording into a vertical Instagram Reel in minutes. Auto cursor follow, click zoom and captions, no video editor. Free for Windows, Chrome and the web.
How to make YouTube Shorts from a screen recording
Make YouTube Shorts from screen recordings: auto-crop to 9:16, follow your cursor, zoom on clicks and add captions. Free tool for Windows, Chrome and web. No editing skills needed.
How to turn a screen recording into a TikTok video
Turn a landscape screen recording into a vertical TikTok video with auto zoom, cursor follow and captions. Free, private, runs on your device. Works for Reels and Shorts too.
How to record your screen in vertical (9:16) video
Record your computer screen in vertical 9:16 for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Cursor follow, click zoom, captions, camera bubble. Free and open source.
